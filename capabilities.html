ENGINEERING SERVICES

Capabilities built around your requirements.

Engineering and product-development support for businesses working on new products, specialised equipment and automation challenges. Scope is defined around the project and required capabilities.

01 / PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Product R&D & Mechanical Design

Explore product concepts, mechanisms and mechanical assemblies grounded in requirements and feasibility.

  • Concept exploration and feasibility discussions
  • CAD parts, assemblies and design iterations
  • Drawings and documentation, as agreed

02 / INTEGRATED SYSTEMS

Robotics & Mechatronics

Develop mechanisms and integrated systems that bring mechanical motion, sensors, electronics and control together.

  • Mechanism and subsystem development
  • Actuator and sensor integration
  • Prototype assembly and functional checks

03 / EMBEDDED TECHNOLOGY

Electronics & Embedded Systems

Prototype electronic and firmware elements for connected devices, instruments and specialised systems.

  • Microcontroller and module integration
  • Sensor interfaces and firmware prototypes
  • PCB design or documentation where agreed

04 / PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

Automation Engineering

Explore custom automation approaches for repetitive tasks, handling requirements and defined workflows.

  • Workflow and requirements review
  • Automation concepts and subsystem design
  • Proof-of-concept prototypes, subject to scope

05 / RAPID ITERATION

3D Printing & Prototyping

Make concepts tangible for fit checks, assembly trials, ergonomic review and early functional exploration.

  • 3D-printed prototype components
  • Assembly and fit-check iterations
  • Prototype refinement based on findings

06 / VERIFICATION

Testing & Engineering Verification

Check defined functions and record observations against agreed requirements. Scope and limitations are agreed in advance.

  • Functional checks and electrical measurements
  • Test observations and issue tracking
  • Verification summaries and next-step recommendations
