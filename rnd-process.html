FROM REQUIREMENTS TO RESULTS

Engineering is more than an idea. It's a process.

Effective product development needs clear requirements, considered design choices, practical implementation and evidence that a solution performs as intended.

01

Discover & define

Understand the problem, operating environment, constraints, budget expectations and success criteria.

Typical outputs: requirements summary, scope, assumptions and acceptance criteria.

02

Design & assess feasibility

Explore concepts, compare practical options and identify major mechanical, electronic, software and integration considerations.

Typical outputs: concepts, CAD or system architecture where appropriate, component considerations and proposed plan.

03

Build & integrate

Develop the agreed prototype or subsystem, assemble components and integrate elements needed for the planned test.

Typical outputs: prototype components, assemblies, firmware or software elements where included in scope.

04

Verify & refine

Check defined functions against agreed requirements, record observations, investigate failures and iterate where budget and schedule allow.

Typical outputs: test observations, issue list, revised prototype and documented limitations.

05

Deliver & hand over

Provide agreed deliverables and explain results, open issues, known limitations and recommended next steps.

Typical outputs: agreed files, documentation, prototype handover and recommendations.

Important: Testing is scoped to the project. A prototype demonstration does not automatically constitute regulatory approval, certification, production qualification or safety validation.
